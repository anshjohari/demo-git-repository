My dearest Didi,

I am not good with words. I never have been — and you know that better than anyone in the world. But today, on your birthday, I am going to try. Because you deserve to hear this, at least once, from me.

I know I was not the brother you perhaps imagined. I made mistakes — more than I should have. There were times I was careless, times I was absent, times I took everything you did for granted without even realising it. And through all of that, you never walked away. You just quietly helped — with school, with life, with everything — without being asked, without expecting anything back.

That is what moves me when I sit and think about it. You always showed up for me, even when I did not know how to show up for you.

I think about how far you are from home. How you carry your world on your own shoulders still, somehow, you stay warm. You stay you. I worry about you, Didi. Even from this distance, I take tension whenever I know things are hard for you. I just never say it out loud — because I am still figuring out how.

But please know this, and keep it with you always: I see you. I love you — even though I've never quite known how to say it, until now.

You are the best person in my life. And I hope this birthday is the beginning of every beautiful thing you have always deserved.

— Your little brother, always  🌙
